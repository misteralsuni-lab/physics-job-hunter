## Q5: EDEXCEL — contamination vs irradiation
- **Contamination:** coffee on shirt → ON body → wash to remove → can spread
- **Irradiation:** standing under lamp → exposed, not made of light → walk away, stops
- Rule: "Inside your body = CONTAMINATION. Outside & walk away = IRRADIATION." Solves 80% of exam errors.
- Show diagrams: person with dust on skin (contamination) vs person near lead-boxed source (irradiation)

## BONUS: "Why green glow in movies?"
- Radium alone ≠ green; pale-blue from air ionisation
- Mixed with zinc sulfide (phosphor) → alpha hits phosphor → green light
- "Radium paint" brand → public linked green to radiation
- 1950s Hollywood: visual shorthand for "dangerous"
- Reality: uranium = silvery-grey, plutonium = dull grey. No element naturally glows sci-fi green.
