### 1.4 Immigration (non-EU teacher visa feasibility)

**Germany — EU Blue Card / employment visa:**
- Teachers are a *regulated profession*; qualification recognition via **ZAB / Anabin** is normally required. [Germany.info Blue Card checklist, HIGH]
- 2025 Blue Card thresholds: **€50,700** standard, **€45,934.20** for shortage ("bottleneck") occupations, new entrants, and IT. Physics/Maths sit in the STEM/bottleneck category, so the lower bar applies. [Germany.info 2025 PDF; Nigeria embassy 2026 note shows ~€45,934–€50,700 band, MEDIUM–HIGH — thresholds shift yearly]
- International schools explicitly sponsor: BBIS "visa support available for long-term commitments"; FIS "will assist with residence and working permits" and covers initial permit cost. [BBIS/FIS, HIGH]
- **Settlement path is strong:** Blue Card → permanent residency after ~33 months (or 21 with German B1); family reunification straightforward. [Make-it-in-Germany, HIGH]

**Czech Republic — Employee Card:**
- Third-country nationals need an **Employee Card** (employer-sponsored residence-for-work permit); the vacancy must be registered with the labour office. [MZV.cz, HIGH]
- ⚠️ Since **1 July 2024**, citizens of **UK, USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Israel, South Korea, Singapore** have *free access to the Czech labour market* (no work permit) — but still need a residence permit. If the candidate holds any of those passports this is a major advantage; otherwise full Employee Card sponsorship applies. [MZV.cz / DZS, HIGH — but candidate nationality unconfirmed, so MEDIUM for applicability]
- LVA explicitly offers "VISA assistance & help with…" in its TES advert. [LVA TES, HIGH]
- Settlement path exists but is slower/smaller than Germany's. [Analyst, MEDIUM]
